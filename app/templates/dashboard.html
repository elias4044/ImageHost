Every image must pass NSFW screening before delivery.

Scan pipeline

NSFW scanning is required. Malware and specialist checks are optional; once enabled, they must also pass. A configured scanner can still be unavailable.

The local model detects exposed nudity. It can miss content and does not determine whether an image is illegal.

Quarantine policy

There is no manual approval or scanner bypass. Failed scans can be retried; blocked images cannot be released.
